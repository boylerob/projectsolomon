⚖️ Weighting System Breakdown

The smart weighting system considers 5 factors:

  • Relevance (35%): Keyword matching and semantic similarity
  • Contextual (25%): Conversation flow and topic continuity
  • Diversity (20%): Avoids repetition and maintains variety
  • User Preference (15%): Matches user's tone and interaction style
  • Temporal (5%): Adapts to conversation length and time of day
